Colour palettes of the editor themes
====================================

GBADE's Monokai, Tokyo Night and Catppuccin Mocha themes use the published
colour values of those projects (crates/gbade/src/themes.rs). Only colour
values are used, no code or other files.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Monokai: colour palette by Wimer Hazenberg, https://monokai.nl
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Credited here as the author of the palette. GBADE does not state a licence
for it: its colour values are used with attribution.
